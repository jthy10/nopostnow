# Code of Conduct

NoPostNow is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and assume
good intent while remaining open to correction. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and community
participation that conflict with these expectations. Serious or sensitive
conduct concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security
advisory channel rather than a public issue.
